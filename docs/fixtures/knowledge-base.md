## Document: Returns Policy

### RET-01 — Standard return window

Unopened, non-defective products may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery. A receipt or order confirmation is required. Products must include their original packaging and accessories. Gift cards are not returnable.

### RET-02 — Opened products and defects

Opened products cannot be returned for change of mind. If a product arrives defective or damaged, contact support within seven calendar days after delivery, even if it has been opened. Support reviews the evidence and determines whether a replacement or refund is appropriate. The assistant cannot approve a return, replacement or refund.

### RET-03 — Return shipping and refund timing

Customers pay return shipping for eligible change-of-mind returns. Haven pays return shipping for an approved defective or damaged-item return. After an eligible returned product is received and inspected, the refund is issued to the original payment method within five to ten business days. The refund is not promised within five to ten days of the initial request.

## Document: Delivery Policy

### SHIP-01 — Delivery regions and charges

Haven delivers only within Pakistan. Standard shipping costs PKR 250 for an order below PKR 5,000 and is free for an order of PKR 5,000 or more. Express shipping costs PKR 600 regardless of order value.

### SHIP-02 — Processing and estimated delivery

Orders are processed within one to two business days before dispatch. Standard delivery usually takes three to five business days after dispatch. Express delivery usually takes one to two business days after dispatch. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed arrival dates.

### SHIP-03 — Tracking and delivery problems

A tracking link is included in the dispatch confirmation. These policy documents do not contain live order locations or current courier events. For a missing, delayed or incorrectly delivered parcel, contact support with the order reference. Never share payment card information or account passwords.

## Document: Warranty Policy

### WAR-01 — Coverage

Haven electronics include a 12-month limited warranty from delivery covering manufacturing defects under normal use. Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs and normal cosmetic wear are excluded. A receipt or order confirmation is required for a claim. Accessories are covered only if their product document explicitly says so.

### WAR-02 — Warranty assessment

Support must assess warranty claims and confirm eligibility before any repair, replacement or refund. The assistant can explain coverage and draft a case but cannot approve a claim. No repair completion time is guaranteed in these documents.

## Document: Product Guide

### PROD-01 — Glow Desk Lamp

The Glow Desk Lamp has three brightness levels and a USB-C power input. A USB-C cable is included; a wall power adapter is not included. Use it indoors in a dry area. It is not waterproof. The lamp has no battery and must remain connected to a suitable power source. No smart-home compatibility is stated in this guide.

### PROD-02 — Trail Bottle

The Trail Bottle holds 750 mL and is made of stainless steel. Wash it by hand; it is not dishwasher-safe or microwave-safe. The guide does not specify how long it keeps liquids hot or cold. Replacement lids are not described in the available documents.

## Document: Support and Privacy FAQ

### SUP-01 — Human support

Human support hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00–18:00 Pakistan Standard Time, excluding public holidays. The target for a first reply is within two business days. This is a target, not a guaranteed resolution time. A local support ticket can be submitted at any time; creating one does not mean an email has been sent.

### SUP-02 — Information for a support case

A support case needs a subject, description and contact email. An order reference is useful for order-related cases but is optional when creating a ticket. Share only information needed for the issue. Do not include passwords, full payment card numbers or banking credentials. The customer reviews the proposed summary and shared conversation excerpts before submitting.

### SUP-03 — Assistant limitations

The assistant answers from published Haven documents. It cannot access live orders, cancel orders, change delivery addresses, approve refunds or warranties, or make purchases. If information is missing or an action requires human approval, it can offer a support ticket. No cancellation cutoff, discount policy or repair price list is provided in these documents.
